## Exact prompt

Use case: identity-preserve / style-transfer
Asset type: one full-bleed 4:5 portrait music artwork preview, no typography.
Input 1 is the edit target and sole source for the person and synthesizer clothing. Input 2 (Old Friend) and input 3 (I Learned the Hard Way) are supporting style references only: borrow their audacious cropping, torn photograph/cut-paper composition, large abrupt ink masses, fine registration lines, and integrated print character; do not import either reference person's face.
Primary request: radically recompose input 1 into ELECTRIC CUT, a tense, bold photographic collage rather than a border or recolor variation. Move much closer: the same photographed man's looming face is about one third of image height, placed asymmetrically; beret nearly reaches the top, his enlarged synthesizer upper torso fills the lower field, lower body cropped away. Retain his recognizable human face with pores, wrinkles, mouth and exact expression, thin glasses, dark beret, earring, warm monochrome photographic lighting and all facial anatomy. Face must remain real photographed human skin, never painted yellow, flat illustrated, robotic, faceted or cubist.
Composition: a sharply oblique black piano plane cuts diagonally through the lower composition, making an assertive wedge against the enlarged synth chest. Preserve recognizable keys, knobs and cables from the source. Boldly interrupt the peripheral shoulders and torso with a few torn photographic fragments displaced a little from the body, large uneven warm-bone negative-space cuts, and abrupt saturated ink fields that actively cut into the silhouette. Interlock these shapes with the subject so this is a new composition, not a intact cutout standing in front of equal decorative stripes. One very faint offset ghost of the head contour, just behind the real head, gives a subtly uncanny atmosphere without a second full face.
Color palette: only rich deep aubergine and saturated sulfur yellow as assertive inks, warm bone paper and dense black; warm sepia monochrome photograph. Big decisively shaped color masses, high contrast, economical confident design. No rainbow, candy colors or neon gradient.
Print treatment: hard misregistration strokes at selected torn edges; a few delicate registration lines; sparse coarse halftone integrated into one shoulder and paper edges. Paper grain remains tactile but do not cover everything in uniform grunge. Keep the face clear and detailed.
Constraints: exactly one artwork, same portrait 4:5 aspect ratio. Full bleed with no enclosing border. No text, letters, logos, watermarks or captions. Strong new compositional flair with preserved identity, not simply the original with new border colors.
