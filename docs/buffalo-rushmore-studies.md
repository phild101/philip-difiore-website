## The Fifth Face — exact prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: one fictional album-art concept, 4:5 portrait, 1600x2000 preferred, image only.
Input image 1 is the subject-identity and print-style reference. Translate this exact buffalo's broad shaggy forehead, sweeping curved horns, heavy-set eyes and broad muzzle into carved granite. Keep its instantly readable distinctive frontal silhouette.
Primary request: THE FIFTH FACE. Create a photographically believable monumental Mount Rushmore granite cliff with the four recognizable presidential stone portraits (Washington, Jefferson, Roosevelt and Lincoln) and this buffalo carved as a FIFTH colossal stone relief integrated into the central cliff, larger than the adjacent presidential heads. All five heads must be visible; buffalo sits among them, not pasted in front. Bison forehead hair becomes sculpted rough granite ridges, horns and broad muzzle hewn from the same weathered stone. Coherent geological join to surrounding cliff, cracks, chisel planes, natural cavities and directional sunlight and shadow. Clearly stone, no live fur, no floating animal.
Composition: dramatic low-angle tightly framed portrait crop; almost whole frame occupied by the rocky carvings, small wedge of sky and a thin dark pine ridge at the lower edge for scale. Buffalo is the dominant central focal point with strong carved eyes and horns legible at thumbnail. Presidential faces remain clear and distinct.
Style: sophisticated forceful analog overprint collage in the exact source artwork's print vocabulary. Deep petrol blue, muted coral, warm bone, and inky black. Rough large torn vertical and diagonal ink plates interact with geological planes and cross selected carvings; worn screenprint edges, selective halftone, offset registration strokes and tactile paper grain. Keep believable photographic stone sculpture under the ink; artistic fictional montage, visually powerful album artwork.
Constraints: one finished portrait image, no text, no titles, no letters, no logo, no UI, no watermark, no border, no extra faces. Preserve the reference buffalo's identity and palette; replace its original plain backdrop with the Mount Rushmore scene.

## Buffalo Monument — exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one standalone image-only art mockup, portrait 4:5, high resolution.
Primary request: BUFFALO MONUMENT — turn the buffalo from the reference into a colossal photorealistic granite bison monument carved directly from Mount Rushmore's rocky mountain. The buffalo is the star, dominating the main ridge center; the presidential stone faces remain small but recognizable architectural fragments together at one lower edge, making the Mount Rushmore connection unmistakable.
Input image 1: identity and overprint style reference only. Preserve its specific broad shaggy head proportions, sweeping hooked horns, huge forehead, low heavy gaze and broad muzzle; reinterpret every surface as carved stone. It is ONE buffalo head.
Scene/backdrop: continuous craggy mountain cliff with the buffalo head, horns, carved fur relief and muzzle physically part of the same granite massif. Lower small Rushmore faces merge naturally into that cliff. Strong realistic cliff cracks, chisel erosion, stone grain, deep relief shadows, dramatic directional sunlight.
Composition/framing: aggressive tight asymmetrical 4:5 portrait; the giant buffalo occupies almost all the image; one horn reaches the upper edge. Imposing near-frontal face slightly angled by the mountain perspective, monumental scale, deep sculpted curls representing shaggy fur, powerful realistic carved eye and muzzle relief. Crop into the surrounding mountain, not a distant landscape postcard.
Style/medium: photographic stone realism as the underlying image, transformed into an artful dramatic screenprinted gig poster overprint. Bold large BROKEN OBLIQUE ink planes, torn-paper interruptions and coarse halftone at selective edges; fine imperfect registration. Maintain clear carved buffalo facial structure through the ink.
Color palette: rust vermilion, rich golden ochre, warm bone and black. Broad intentional areas of clean ink and bone negative space, angular diagonal color interaction.
Constraints: stone sculpture rooted in the mountain, never a living fur head pasted or floating above a hill. Buffalo remains dominant; presidents small but legible. No text, letters, logos, captions, frames, UI, watermarks. No evenly spaced stripes, no allover indiscriminate grunge, no novelty postcard composition. One finished image, no montage.

## Mountain Ghost — exact prompt

Use case: compositing
Asset type: image-only soundtrack artwork concept, MOUNTAIN GHOST.
Input image 1 is the source buffalo portrait and overprint aesthetic reference. Preserve this animal's photographic curly forehead, clear soulful eyes, heavy textured muzzle, and curved horns. Transform it into a haunting surreal photographic double exposure where the enormous buffalo emerges directly from Mount Rushmore's granite cliff, mountain and animal as two exposures of the same form.
Composition: one unified 4:5 portrait, tight monumental crop. Buffalo horns and gaze are the first-read dominant subject, fully legible and arresting. The four recognizable Mount Rushmore presidential granite carvings—Washington, Jefferson, Roosevelt, Lincoln—appear as a coherent sculpted ridge across the buffalo's lower shoulder/chest and rock strata, with granite fissures and fur dissolving seamlessly into each other. Give the presidents a readable secondary scale, never tiny thumbnails, and never cover the buffalo eyes or muzzle. The animal and mountain occupy almost the whole frame. Ghostly photo-emulsion blending, not a literal head pasted onto a postcard. Convincing photographic fur, horn, skin, chiseled stone, and deep black crevices.
Print language: retain the reference's bold distressed analog overprinting, now in midnight cobalt and indigo, pale bone, and restrained burnt coral accents. Large asymmetrical ripped-paper color fields and one broken circular arc interact dramatically with the subject; occasional selective halftone, worn ink, slight film registration ghosts. Keep focal eyes optically clear. Strong dark-light silhouette, dense tactile detail, elegant large graphic forms.
Mood: solemn, eerie, mythic, dreamlike photographic collage. No cartoon, caricature, comic styling, glossy 3D, generic painterly rendering, scattered duplicates, collage grid, tiny picture tiles, text, title, lettering, logo, UI, watermark, or decorative white border. Generate one finished image, 4:5 portrait.
